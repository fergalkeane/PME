Our approach

The road to organisational excellence takes many forms.

Unfortunately many organisations are still focused on fire fighting rather than root cause analysis. Performance Management Excellence is committed to working with organisations to achieve superior performance.

The focus may be on the performance of an individual, a team, a function, or the whole organisation. Whatever your area of focus, we provide experience and practical expertise to support you to maximise performance.

Services

Practical expertise, delivered where performance is won.

Six connected disciplines. Engaged individually or as a full programme, tailored to your organisation.

01

Performance Improvement

Support of leadership teams to identify performance gaps and to create and implement performance improvement plans.

Poor performance is often symptomatic of hidden problems. We conduct root cause diagnostic analysis and verify that supporting structures and processes reinforce improvement strategies.

02

Performance Goals & Strategies

Assistance in developing and bringing clarity to performance goals and strategies at organisational and functional level.

We focus on how goals cascade and are adopted throughout the organisation, linking development plans and outcomes directly to strategy.

03

Mentoring & Facilitation

Provision of mentoring and facilitation support to work groups and management teams in the development of strategies and objectives.

Support across mentoring, facilitation, group dynamics, conflict resolution and team building.

04

Self Sustaining Processes

Analysis of existing processes and development of new processes to sustain and drive ongoing performance improvement.

Short-term wins propel change forward, but improvements often fail to take root. The key to sustainment is robust supporting processes.

05

Lean Implementation & Sustainment

Provision of expertise in Lean implementation based on practical hands-on experience.

Results orientated and grounded in solid theoretical knowledge, with a flexible approach based on the unique needs of each organisation.

06

People Development & Training

Training and development support clearly aligned to business goals, customised to individual organisational requirements.

  • Performance Management
  • Leadership & Management
  • Personal Management
  • Lean Implementation

We believe training should be grounded in organisational needs. To be effective it must result in behavioural change within the workplace.

Who we are

A small, flexible consultancy and training organisation.

PME Ltd. tailors its approach and support to the specific needs of client organisations working across both the service and manufacturing sectors. At any one time we focus on a small client base in order to provide exceptional customer service.

Martin Sheehan

Founder · Lean Black Belt

Martin has over 25 years’ experience across a number of business sectors, holding senior positions in Lean and Process Excellence, Learning and Development, Organisational Development, Change Management, Supply Chain Management and Operations. He is passionate about people development, with a particular focus on Lean Systems, Leadership/Management Development and Operational Excellence. A certified Master Trainer and Lean Black Belt, he also holds an MSc in People and Organisational Development.

Paul Nevin

Business Consultant

He has worked with multinational corporations and indigenous companies across Irish, UK and emerging European markets. Paul has successfully supported a number of start-ups with product development. A graduate of DCU Dublin Business School, he has 15 years’ Sales and General Management experience.
